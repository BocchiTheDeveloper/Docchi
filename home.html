Hello, welcome to my app. As a college student living on my own, I often struggle with responsibilities. I procrastinate a lot and I sometimes wish I could do something about it, which is why I built this app. It's just a simple to-do list app, and I know there's tons of them online but it just feels better if I made one myself. Anyways, hope you find this useful!

-Eco

App guide:
Home Screen: This is where you can track your progress/completed tasks. Complete daily tasks every day to highlight a day in the progress calendar.
Daily Tasks: Daily tasks reset daily. You can choose to add, check off, or delete any tasks you've listed down.
Normal Tasks: Normal tasks don't reset daily. Instead, you have the option to set a due date for your tasks.
